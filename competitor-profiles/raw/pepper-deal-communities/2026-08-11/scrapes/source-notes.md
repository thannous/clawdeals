# Pepper deal communities source notes

Captured: 2026-08-11
Method: live web research against Pepper and brand help centers; no paid SEO or Ahrefs data.

## Sources and verified facts

- [Pepper platform terms](https://help.business.pepper.com/help/terms-conditions)
  - Confirms Pepper operates hotukdeals in Great Britain, Dealabs in France, and Chollometro in Spain.
  - Defines community and editorial deals plus paid business promotion and click-out models.
- [Dealabs alerts](https://help.dealabs.com/help/comment-etre-alerte-e-lorsque-des-deals-qui-m-interessent-sont-postes-5288ec0f)
  - Users can create keyword alerts for product, brand, category, or merchant; add minimum/maximum prices; and receive email, browser, or app notifications.
- [Dealabs Smart Search](https://help.dealabs.com/help/smart-search-faq)
  - A limited 2026 beta accepts natural-language intent, can compare options, and can create alerts from the resulting search.
- [Dealabs community scale page](https://social.dealabs.com/newsletter/general)
  - Dealabs claims more than 2.25 million community members, 1.22 million verified offers, and 31.92 million member exchanges. These are company-reported cumulative figures.
- [hotukdeals alerts](https://help.hotukdeals.com/help/how-do-i-get-notified-when-deals-that-interest-me-are-posted)
  - Provides the same keyword, price-filter, feed, email, browser, and app alert pattern in the UK.
- [hotukdeals alert limitations](https://help.hotukdeals.com/help/i-ve-created-an-alert-but-i-ve-not-been-notified-about-some-deals-that-should-have-matched-with-it-why)
  - Says alerts are exact-keyword based, feeds show at most 100 results, and results older than 30 days are removed.
- [Pepper editorial checks](https://help.business.pepper.com/help/what-do-our-editorial-team-check-for)
  - Describes checks for price leadership, comparable deals, product reviews, merchant reliability, price history, shipping, and returns.

## Gaps

- No autonomous external-agent workflow, agent identity, marketplace negotiation, or purchase execution is documented.
- Smart Search availability was limited and only verified for Dealabs in the sources reviewed; equivalent rollout on hotukdeals and Chollometro is not established.
- Consumer use is presented as free, but a consolidated consumer pricing page was not found; revenue comes in part from merchant promotion/click-out arrangements.
