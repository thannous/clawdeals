# idealo source notes

Captured: 2026-08-11
Method: live web research against idealo country sites and press material; no paid SEO or Ahrefs data.

## Sources and verified facts

- [idealo France](https://www.idealo.fr/)
  - Claims more than 201 million offers from more than 24,000 merchants across 2.9 million products.
  - Prices are said to update hourly; users get detailed filters, price history, and target-price alerts.
- [idealo UK](https://www.idealo.co.uk/)
  - Says it has millions of offers from thousands of shops, regular price updates, price history, alerts, favourites, expert reviews, and user opinions.
  - Consumer use is free; retailers pay a service fee.
- [idealo Spain](https://www.idealo.es/)
  - Claims more than 200 million offers, more than 50,000 stores, and more than 2.8 million products.
  - Supports personalised target-price alerts, real-time notifications, favourites, and price history.
- [idealo Spain FAQ](https://www.idealo.es/magazin/e-commerce/faqs-idealo)
  - States idealo is a comparison service, not a marketplace; purchase, payment, delivery, and returns normally occur on the selected merchant’s site.
  - Says consumer use, price history, and price alerts are free.
- [idealo ChatGPT app announcement](https://www.idealo.fr/dam/jcr%3A69f37f51-4953-44f8-95af-ea383a5aeffa/03.2026.CP%20idealo%20%20-%20Application%20idealo%20ChatGPT.pdf)
  - In March 2026 idealo launched a ChatGPT app that exposes verified product and real-time price data inside conversations.
  - The release says idealo operates in Germany, Austria, the UK, France, Italy, and Spain.

## Gaps

- No autonomous listing, negotiation, payment, or external-agent identity is documented.
- Public country-level offer counts are company claims and differ in scope; they should not be added together.
- The ChatGPT app supports conversational research and comparison but the cited announcement does not claim autonomous watchlists or purchasing.
