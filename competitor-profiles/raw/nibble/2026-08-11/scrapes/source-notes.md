# Nibble source notes

Captured: 2026-08-11
Method: live web research against Nibble product pages and documents; no paid SEO or Ahrefs data.

## Sources and verified facts

- [Nibble homepage](https://nibbletechnology.com/)
  - Positions Nibble primarily as an AI negotiation platform for procurement, including tail-spend campaigns, ad hoc negotiations, RFQs, price lists, and API-driven custom cases.
  - Describes integration with Coupa and SAP Ariba and an API suite.
  - Claims pricing functions are controlled outside the LLM and says it has handled more than 2 million automated negotiations. This is company-reported usage.
  - Pricing is not public; the call to action is to request a sales call.
- [How it works](https://nibbletechnology.com/how-it-works/)
  - Uses controlled pricing algorithms, negotiation techniques, and human-centered conversation design.
  - Lists Coupa, SAP Ariba, Zip, Sievo, or other procurement platforms as data/workflow integrations.
- [E-commerce one-pager](https://nibbletechnology.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/OnePager_2025_Ecommerce-1.pdf)
  - Describes 24/7 AI negotiation for targeted discounts and custom trade pricing.
  - Lists Salesforce, Adobe Commerce, Shopify Plus, and BigCommerce.
- [E-commerce deployment examples](https://blog.nibbletechnology.com/3-ways-to-use-ai-negotiation-in-ecommerce/)
  - Describes product-page negotiation, targeted offers, and integrations/plugins for Magento, BigCommerce, Shopify, or an API.
- [Orchestration integration](https://blog.nibbletechnology.com/how-ai-negotiation-fits-in-your-orchestration-layer/)
  - Describes structured context and policy constraints flowing into Nibble, controlled concessions and escalations during negotiation, and results/audit trails returning to orchestration for approvals and downstream actions.

## Gaps

- No consumer marketplace, buyer watchlist, peer-to-peer listing, or external personal-agent identity is documented.
- Country and currency coverage are not clearly enumerated on the reviewed pages.
- Human approval is configurable through client workflow/orchestration rather than a universal per-consumer approval promise.
