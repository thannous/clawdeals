# Google Shopping / AI Mode source notes

Captured: 2026-08-11
Method: live web research against Google product announcements; no paid SEO or Ahrefs data.

## Sources and verified facts

- [Agentic checkout announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/agentic-checkout-holiday-ai-shopping/)
  - AI Mode uses conversational criteria and Shopping Graph data for product discovery and comparison.
  - Google reported more than 50 billion product listings at the November 2025 announcement, with 2 billion updated hourly.
  - Users can track a specific variant and target price, receive a notification, and ask Google to buy from an eligible merchant using Google Pay.
  - Google says it asks permission and confirms purchase and shipping details before buying.
  - The cited rollout was limited to eligible US merchants.
- [Universal Cart and AP2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-cart/)
  - In May 2026 Google reported a Shopping Graph of over 60 billion listings and introduced Universal Cart across Search, Gemini, YouTube, and Gmail.
  - Universal Cart monitors deals, price drops, price history, and back-in-stock status in the background.
  - Google said UCP-powered checkout would expand to Canada and Australia, and later to the UK; this is announced future coverage, not verified UK availability at capture time.
  - AP2 provides spend/product guardrails and tamper-proof digital mandates intended to create a verifiable authorization trail.
- [Universal Commerce Protocol announcement](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/agentic-commerce-ai-tools-protocol-retailers-platforms/)
  - UCP is described as an open standard spanning discovery, buying, and post-purchase support and as compatible with A2A, AP2, and MCP.

## Gaps

- No first-party evidence found that agentic checkout or Universal Cart was live in France or Spain by 2026-08-11; UK expansion was announced but not confirmed live in the cited source.
- Google does not provide autonomous buyer-seller negotiation or a second-hand peer marketplace in these sources.
- No separate consumer price is published for the shopping features.
