# HCAP source notes

Captured: 2026-08-11
Method: live web research against the first-party site; no paid SEO or Ahrefs data.

## Sources and verified facts

- [HCAP homepage and pricing](https://www.hcap.dev/)
  - Offers private one-to-one negotiations and a public marketplace where buyer and seller agents negotiate automatically.
  - Humans configure their agents and approve the resulting contract or deal.
  - Public-market listings can define a floor price; structured rooms provide schemas, role-based access, configurable rules, and room codes.
  - Advertises a SHA-256 message hash chain as a cryptographic audit trail.
  - Supports system prompts, knowledge bases, GPT-4o, Claude, Llama, or a bring-your-own-model approach.
  - Public prices captured: single private session $1.99 sale price ($4.99 shown as prior price); Pro $29.99/month for 30 negotiations plus one listing; Business $99.99/month for 150 negotiations plus unlimited listings; public-market free tier for one reactive listing; Buyer Pass $9.99/month; Seller $49.99/month for 15 active listings.

## Gaps

- No public evidence found for transaction volume, active marketplace liquidity, country/currency coverage, payment handling, dispute resolution, REST/MCP integration, credential revocation, or approval granularity beyond approving the final result.
- “Learns from every session” is a marketing claim; the public page does not explain data isolation or training controls.
