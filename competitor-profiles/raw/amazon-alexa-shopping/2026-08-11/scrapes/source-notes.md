# Amazon Alexa for Shopping source notes

Captured: 2026-08-11
Method: live web research against About Amazon product pages; no paid SEO or Ahrefs data.

## Sources and verified facts

- [Alexa for Shopping capabilities](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-rufus-ai-assistant-personalized-shopping-features)
  - Rufus was renamed Alexa for Shopping on 2026-05-13.
  - It supports conversational discovery, comparisons, deal finding, 30/90-day price history, target-price alerts, and Auto-Buy for Prime members.
  - Auto-Buy checks prices, uses the default payment and shipping details when criteria are met, and gives the customer a 24-hour cancellation window after purchase.
  - Amazon says it can surface off-Amazon products through Buy for Me or direct merchant links.
- [Price-history availability](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-check-amazon-price-history)
  - In June 2026 Amazon said price history was available in the US, UK, Canada, and India, with 365-day history rolling out in the US, UK, and India.
  - Amazon reported more than 50 million customers had used price history; this is company-reported adoption.
- [Agentic shopping portfolio](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-agentic-ai-gen-ai-shopping)
  - Amazon described Alexa for Shopping as remembering preferences, setting alerts, purchasing on the user’s behalf, and searching on- and off-Amazon brands.
  - Buy for Me can purchase selected products on external brand sites when they are not sold in Amazon’s store.

## Gaps

- The current sources do not confirm Auto-Buy or Buy for Me availability in France, Spain, or the UK. Only price-history coverage is explicitly confirmed for the UK.
- The agent is bound to Amazon’s consumer account and commerce surfaces; no external-agent identity or MCP/REST marketplace access is documented.
- It does not provide peer-to-peer listing or buyer-seller negotiation in the cited sources.
- No separate feature price is published; Auto-Buy is stated to require Prime.
