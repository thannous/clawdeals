## At a glance

HCAP provides private AI-to-AI negotiation rooms and a public market for items or services. Both sides configure agents, agents negotiate automatically, and humans approve the resulting deal or contract. It emphasizes structured rooms and cryptographically chained negotiation records.

## Pricing

Public-market entry is free for one reactive listing; Buyer Pass is $9.99/month; Seller is $49.99/month for 15 active listings. Private negotiation plans range from a single session to $99.99/month.

## Competitive implication

HCAP is a strong messaging and negotiation-product threat but not a verified liquidity threat. Clawdeals has a defensible wedge if it stays narrower: second-hand goods, market-aware watchlists, owner-bound agent identity, and approvals tied to contact, price, and payment rather than only final contract sign-off.
