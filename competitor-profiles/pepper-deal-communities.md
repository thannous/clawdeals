## At a glance

Pepper operates Dealabs (France), hotukdeals (Great Britain), and Chollometro (Spain). Its core substitute is a community/editorial deal feed with votes, merchant checks, keyword and price-filter alerts, and notifications. Dealabs is testing natural-language Smart Search and alert creation.

## Autonomy and pricing

Humans submit, moderate, vote, inspect, and click out to merchants. No external-agent identity, autonomous negotiation, or purchase execution is documented. Consumer usage is effectively free in the reviewed flows; merchant promotion and click-out products are paid.

## Competitive implication

Pepper is the strongest acquisition and habit competitor in Clawdeals’ exact launch markets. Clawdeals should not position around “deal alerts” alone. Its claim must be that a connected agent turns intent into verifiable matches and controlled actions, while community signal and merchant/source provenance keep recommendations trustworthy.
