## At a glance

Alexa for Shopping offers conversational discovery, comparisons, deal finding, price history, target-price alerts, and Prime-member Auto-Buy. It can also surface and sometimes buy selected products on external brand sites.

## Autonomy and coverage

Auto-Buy executes after a standing user rule is met, then offers 24-hour cancellation. UK price history is verified; current Auto-Buy/Buy for Me coverage is not confirmed for Clawdeals’ three markets. It does not provide peer-to-peer listings or agent-to-agent negotiation.

## Competitive implication

Amazon sets the consumer expectation that a watchlist can become a standing purchase mandate. Clawdeals’ human-approval promise must therefore be expressed as an explicit autonomy ladder, not a generic “human in the loop” claim. The differentiated territory remains open-market, second-hand, cross-agent negotiation and revocable external-agent access.
