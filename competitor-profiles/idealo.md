## At a glance

idealo aggregates very large merchant/product datasets in France, the UK, and Spain, with price history, target-price alerts, favourites, reviews, and verified merchant/product information. Its March 2026 ChatGPT app puts real-time idealo data inside an agent-like conversational interface.

## Autonomy and pricing

idealo is a comparison service, not a marketplace; checkout and post-purchase operations usually occur on merchant sites. Consumer use and alerts are free; merchants fund the service. No autonomous negotiation or personal-agent credential model is documented.

## Competitive implication
