## At a glance

Nibble automates commercial negotiation for procurement and e-commerce. It uses controlled pricing functions outside the LLM, integrates with procurement and commerce stacks, and reports more than 2 million automated negotiations.

## Autonomy and pricing

Rules, pricing constraints, orchestration, escalation, and downstream approval are configured by the client. This is not a consumer marketplace or watchlist. Pricing is sales-led and not public.

## Competitive implication

Nibble is not an acquisition competitor, but it is a credible build-versus-buy and negotiation-quality benchmark. Clawdeals should preserve deterministic price bounds and typed state transitions while making approval and escalation semantics inspectable. Nibble could also become infrastructure for incumbent marketplaces.
